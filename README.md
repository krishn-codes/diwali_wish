# Krishna Sharma | A Cinematic Diwali Experience

An ultra-luxurious, cinematic, and interactive Diwali greeting web experience created for family and friends. Faithfully crafted based on the Diwali UI visual direction with high-end motion design, spatial lighting, interactive 3D elements, ambient audio, and responsive architecture.

---

## ✨ Features & Visual Highlights

### 1. Page 1: Home (The Grand Hero Experience)
- **Cinematic Midnight Atmosphere**: Deep royal purple (`#160D2B`), midnight black (`#080611`), and rich violet (`#29113D`) gradients.
- **Illuminated Indian Palace**: Grand Rajput/Mughal architectural spires, chhatris, illuminated jharokhas, and temple ghats lit by thousands of golden festive lamps.
- **Glowing Full Moon & Luminous Sky**: Soft moonlight, ambient golden clouds, twinkling stardust, and organic drifting lanterns across depth layers.
- **Sacred Urli Diya & Calm Reflective Water**: An ornate brass carved Urli diya with realistic multi-layer flame flickering, accompanying blooming pink lotuses, and shimmering water reflection ripples.
- **Interactive Blessings**: Clicking the sacred Urli Diya showers golden spark confetti and blessings across the screen.
- **Glorious Calligraphy Typography**: Sweeping metallic gold gradient and light shine on *"Happy Diwali"*, heartfelt blessings, and Krishna Sharma's signature.
- **About Diwali Section**: Sacred lotus divider, poetic essence of Deepavali, illuminated Ayodhya ghats silhouette, and an animated *"Explore Wishes →"* CTA.

### 2. Page 2: Wishes (Sacred Blessings & Lord Krishna)
- **Diwali Wishes Grid**: 4 floating glassmorphic golden cards with 3D tilt, radial hover spotlight, and miniature glowing brass diyas casting warm amber light on each card.
- **Copy Wish Feature**: Relatives can copy any heartfelt blessing with a single click to share with loved ones.
- **Lord Krishna Illustration**: A divine, golden line-art illustration of Lord Krishna playing the flute (*Bansuri*), adorned with the peacock feather (*Mor Pankh*), jewels, and celestial musical light trails.
- **Maa Lakshmi Blessing**: Dedicated personalized blessing for wealth, peace, and eternal prosperity.
- **Smooth Navigation**: One-click *"Back to Home"* button and glass pill switcher.

### 3. Ambient Systems & Motion Design
- **Initial Cinematic Loading**: Blooming lotus motif, diya flame ignition, and smooth golden progress reveal.
- **Canvas Fireworks Engine**: Realistic physics, trailing sparks, ambient bursts, and interactive click-to-launch fireworks anywhere on the screen.
- **Festive Music Synthesizer**: Built-in ambient Indian raga soundscape (Yaman / Bhupali scales) using Web Audio API with volume toggle.
- **"Spread Love" Trigger**: Top navigation heart button spreads celebratory rose and gold confetti.

- **Tailwind CSS**

- **Lucide React**

# Build for production
npm run build

# Preview production build
npm run preview
